If you did not eat or sleep, it would take \(x\) days to watch all available episodes of Critical Role’s main campaigns.

\[\begin{aligned} x &= \frac{453 + 566 + 508 + 148}{24} \\ &= \frac{1675}{24} \\ &= 69.79167 \\ &\approx 70 \end{aligned}\]

It would take nearly 70 days, or 10 weeks.
